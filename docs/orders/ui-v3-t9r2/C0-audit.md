**Method.** Three trees were read, never modified: BASE = origin/main + PR #64 merged cleanly (231b0f4, a scratch worktree), UINEXT2 = PR #47's tip a92c949 (stacked on PR #46, based on the integration train), C6 = PR #59's tip 386f99d. Full tables: [C0-audit-inventory.md](C0-audit-inventory.md) (537 inventory rows: 289 exist · 156 partial · 46 broken · 46 missing).

## 1 · Verdict

The base is sound and the ORDER is mostly unbuilt. The v2 shell (ShellV2, one pure keyboard reducer, the C5 row-budget solver, typed CHAIN views, the DEMOS card and the wizard on #47, the HANDS board on #64) is real, tested and worth keeping — tsc is clean and the full suite is green on BASE. Against the ORDER's eight checkpoints, nothing is complete: the visual law is a different palette, the tab set is six not eight, two of the four status-line fields have no data source anywhere, CHAIN types cover 2% of the receipts actually on the chain, DEMOS rows do not come from seals, HANDS reads a different file and cannot send, ENGINE ROOM has one of seven rows, two incompatible `timmy init` wizards sit on two open PRs, and the film frames are hash-bound but unsigned, header-less in places and never a 20-second cast. Of the eight demo routes, one (eight hands) is reachable from the keyboard today, and only with a private board present; two (materialize, factory day) have no definition anywhere in the tree or on the chain. One finding cuts across everything: the shipped `timmy` bin (timmy.ts) forwards only a whitelist of verbs to src/cli.ts, so `timmy cockpit`, `timmy privacy`, `timmy engine`, `timmy clip`, `timmy status` and `timmy swarm` — and any future `timmy demo run`, `timmy init` or `timmy arsenal` — are unreachable from the installed command; every "reachable" statement in this audit that names a CLI verb holds only for `npx tsx src/cli.ts`.
